      Chimera

      You are a head. Everything you eat becomes your body, and every creature grows its own shape. Swallow smaller creatures whole, and if a big one swallows you, hope you are big enough to keep your head.

      Eat
      Your head and your whole body eat food and grey cells, and every crumb feeds whoever owns the cell that touched it. Your genome decides the shape: legs, claws, fins, spikes, a long tail or a forked one.
      Absorb
      Touch anything 25% smaller and it becomes part of you. Touch anything 25% bigger and it takes you.
      Two heads
      A chimera has two seats. Heads already seated keep them. Any other head it swallows is eaten, and its cells are split 50/50 between the two pilots.
      Bite
      Close in size? Nobody gets absorbed. Heads bite cells off each other's bodies instead.
      Break
      Space breaks you away from a chimera with every cell you own. If a bigger creature grabs you, you have a moment to hit Space and slip free.

      Mouse: steer · Space / E / right click: break away

      Prototype: the other heads are bots with temperaments. Loyal ones follow their pilot, greedy ones chase food, traitors walk out with their cells.
